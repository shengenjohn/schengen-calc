Refund Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

In short: every account includes 3 free trips, so you can try the calculator before paying. Paid subscriptions are not normally refundable once you start using them, but we always put right billing mistakes and faults. You can cancel at any time to stop future payments.

1. Try before you buy

Every account can add up to 3 trips free of charge, with no card needed. We encourage you to use them to make sure the Service suits you before you subscribe.

2. Refunds are handled by Paddle

Paddle, our Merchant of Record, handles all orders and refunds for paid plans. Refunds are given in line with Paddle's Refund Policy and Buyer Terms, which set out the rights that apply in your country.

If you live in the UK or EU, you normally have 14 days after a purchase to change your mind. Because the Service is digital and available straight away, that right ends once you agree at checkout that your access can start immediately and you then start using it. In some cases the law may give you rights beyond this, and Paddle's Refund Policy explains them.

3. When we will always refund

Whatever the circumstances, we will make sure you get your money back if:

  • you were charged twice, or charged the wrong amount;
  • a subscription renewed after you had cancelled it;
  • a fault on our side meant you could not use the paid features, and we could not fix it within a reasonable time.

Please contact us within 14 days of the payment concerned.

4. How to request a refund

  • Go to paddle.net and look up your purchase; or
  • use the link in your Paddle receipt or confirmation email.

If you have any difficulty, email support@bestschengencalc.com and we will help. Refunds go back to your original payment method. Your bank or card provider may take a few days to show the money. Once a refund is issued, your paid access ends.

5. How to cancel

Use the "Manage subscription" link in your Paddle confirmation email, or go to paddle.net. Cancelling stops future payments, and you keep paid access until the end of the billing period you have already paid for.

6. Subscriptions paid through Square

If your checkout was provided by Square, Paddle cannot manage that subscription. Email support@bestschengencalc.com to cancel or to ask about a refund, and we will deal with it directly on the same basis as this policy.

7. Your legal rights

Nothing in this policy affects your legal rights. If the Service is faulty or not as described, you may be entitled to a repair, a replacement or a refund.
