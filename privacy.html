3. Cookies and storage on your device

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. The Service uses your browser's local storage and session storage for things it needs in order to work:

  • keeping you logged in, and remembering the accounts you use on that device;
  • keeping a copy of your trips on that device;
  • the optional PIN for switching between accounts;
  • remembering that you dismissed a banner.

Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, may also set strictly necessary security cookies. Because this information stays on the device, please log out on any shared device. You can clear it at any time through your browser settings, though you may then need to log in again.

4. Who receives your data

  • Paddle, our Merchant of Record, handles checkout, payments, tax, invoices and related support. Paddle is an independent controller of the data it collects; see its privacy notice.
  • Square processes payments for subscriptions started through a Square checkout.
  • Cloudflare provides our website hosting, database and security services.
  • Cloudflare Email Routing and Google (Gmail) receive and store emails sent to our support address.
  • Resend sends our password reset emails.
  • Professional advisers, such as an accountant, where needed.
  • Authorities, courts or regulators, where the law requires it or where needed to protect legal rights.

5. International transfers

We are based in the UK, and some of our providers process data in other countries, including the United States. Where data leaves the UK or EEA for a country without an adequacy decision, we rely on an approved safeguard, such as the UK-US Data Bridge, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum, or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. Email us if you would like more information.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account and trip data: while your account is open, and deleted within 30 days of your request to close it, unless we need to keep something for a legal reason.
  • Subscription and tax records: for 6 years after the end of the relevant tax year.
  • Waitlist details: until the plan launches or you ask to be removed, and for no more than 24 months.
  • Support messages: for up to 2 years after the matter is closed.
  • Technical logs: for the short periods set by our service providers.

7. Your rights

Under UK data protection law, and the EU GDPR where it applies, you have the right to:

  • access your personal data;
  • have inaccurate data rectified;
  • have your data erased;
  • restrict how we use your data;
  • receive your data in a portable form;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on it.

To use any of these rights, email support@bestschengencalc.com. There is normally no charge, and we will respond within one month. We may ask you to confirm your identity first.

You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, or to your local data protection authority if you live in the EU. We would welcome the chance to help first.

8. Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including encrypted HTTPS connections, hashed passwords and server-side access controls. No online service can be completely secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

9. Children

The Service is not aimed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If you think a child has given us data, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy and will show the new date on this page. We will tell you about significant changes.
